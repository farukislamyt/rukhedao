# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

RukheDao aims to maintain a respectful, constructive, and safe project community. Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Discuss technical disagreements in good faith.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Respect privacy and do not expose personal, confidential, or security-sensitive information.
- Focus feedback on the work, implementation, and evidence rather than individuals.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, hate speech, intimidation, threats, doxxing, malicious disclosure of private information, and repeated disruptive conduct.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.

For sensitive or security-related concerns, use the process described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces maintained for RukheDao.
